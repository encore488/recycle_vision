# QA verdicts — historical

`verdicts*.json` grade an **earlier** detector against two development images:
one conveyor photograph and one of household items on a carpet.

**Do not cite these as results.** Two images cannot support a rate, and the
vocabulary was tuned while looking at them, so they measure a fit rather than
a performance. They are kept because they record how early decisions were
made, which is worth being able to check.

The project's measurements come from a held-out split of WaRP — 522 plant
images the model never saw. See [../docs/DATA_CARD.md](../docs/DATA_CARD.md)
and the Milestone 4 rows in [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).
